Use this skill for a single analyte measured at several known concentrations when the response is the distance from a current peak or trough to its baseline. The fixed Python 3.11 helper extracts traceable responses; the bundled `scripts/origin_task_styled.py` runner performs the final linear regression and creates real Origin workbooks, graphs, PDF/PNG, and an OPJU. Do not present a Python-only calculation as an Origin result.

## Decide the measurement before running

Inspect source files and establish the potential and current columns, units, concentrations, oxidation peak versus reduction trough, peak search window, and baseline definition. Use one consistent, physically justified peak window and baseline rule for all concentrations. Check the raw curves visually before choosing a window; do not search each concentration at a different potential just to obtain a calibration line. For a cyclic voltammogram, select one scan branch with `row_slice` before peak analysis. Ask only if these scientific choices cannot be inferred from the data or the user's instructions. Do not silently smooth, delete outliers, force the intercept to zero, or select a linear range for a better R². If the source measures current density (for example A/cm²), label K as current-density sensitivity and do not divide by electrode area again.

### Evidence-based baseline choice

Before finalizing `baseline.windows`, read [baseline selection](references/baseline-selection.md). Overlay the raw curves and any analyte-free blank with the proposed peak interval, anchor intervals, and resulting baseline. Use a constant baseline only for an approximately flat local background; use the two-flank linear rule only when both peak-free flanks support a local straight background. If the background curves, a flank contains a peak tail or neighboring process, or small defensible anchor shifts materially change the response, stop the standard helper and define a validated alternative. Record the selected rule and its anchor-sensitivity check in `baseline_choice.md`; never choose anchors or a baseline model by the best calibration R².

The helper supports a constant baseline from one anchor window or a straight baseline through the mean points of two windows flanking the peak. It searches the maximum of the baseline-corrected signal in the specified peak window; for a trough it reverses the sign so the reported response `delta_current` is the positive peak-to-baseline distance. It records the signed current change too. Every candidate must be inside the search window, visibly prominent above both window shoulders, at least five times the baseline-anchor RMS, and within 0.03 V of the median candidate potential across concentrations by default. These are predeclared screening thresholds, not proof of chemical identity. The helper writes `peak_quality.json` and candidate `measurements.csv`; if any curve fails or peak positions do not align, it does **not** fit K or launch Origin. Never relax thresholds or move the window merely to obtain a fit; inspect the curves and ask for an independently justified measurement rule or better data. These rules do not cover every electrochemical protocol; if a blank subtraction, overlapping-peak fit, curved baseline, or a different definition is needed, define and validate that method for the task.

Fit `delta_current = K × concentration + b` on the mean response at each concentration, unweighted and with a free intercept. Report K with current/concentration units, its standard error, intercept, R², fitting range, and all replicates. Preserve the sign of K; give `abs(K)` separately if a positive magnitude is desired. If electrode area is supplied, also report K divided by area, with current/concentration/cm² units. A single replicate has no estimable SD: leave it blank rather than recording zero.

## Several resolved peaks

When the overlaid concentration series shows several separated candidate peaks or troughs at distinct potentials, enumerate **all** of them before fitting. Screen each with its own fixed peak window and independently justified baseline, applied consistently across the specified concentrations. The helper accepts one peak per manifest: make a separate manifest, quality report, Origin fit, and case folder for every feature that passes the unchanged checks. For every passing feature, complete the entire existing single-peak workflow independently inside its own case folder. Deliver the same full single-peak package and figure style in each folder: editable Origin OPJU; Origin calibration PNG and PDF; reference-style calibration SVG and PNG; raw-curve and baseline review; manifest, baseline choice, summary, measurement and calibration tables, processed curves, analysis, and Origin QA log. Apply the same screening, fitting, annotation, and visual verification rules to every folder. If only one feature qualifies, follow the original single-peak delivery unchanged. The multi-peak extension adds case folders; it does not replace or change any per-feature figure format, figure style, or output type. Do not keep only the first, tallest, or best-R² peak. Preserve failed candidates as quality results without a K. If peaks overlap or lack clean baseline flanks, use a separately validated resolution method rather than treating them as independent.

Report each passing feature's signed Origin K, standard error, units, and fit range in its own summary. Compare sensitivity by |K| only when the current or current-density convention, concentration units, sample levels, fit range, and response definition are comparable. Number **all qualifying peak folders** by the verified |K| point estimate, with peak potential and branch/direction in each name, for example `灵敏度排名01_HVA_氧化峰+0p140V`, `灵敏度排名02_HVA_氧化峰+0p320V`. The folder names convey the ordering; keep the numerical K values in the separate results. This ranking describes point estimates, not a statistically significant difference; equal |K| at the reported precision may share a rank. Use neutral peak names when K values cannot be compared or a native Origin fit has not been verified.

Origin reports contain absolute paths. For multiple peaks, use scratch `--prepare-only` runs only to provisionally order the final folder names, then run and verify native Origin fits inside those folders. Confirm the final names agree with the Origin |K| values; if not, recreate the affected runs under correct names rather than merely renaming folders and breaking recorded paths.

## Default calibration figure style

Match the bundled [reference calibration image](assets/reference_calibration.png) in **both** the editable Origin graph and every delivered calibration figure: white canvas, complete black rectangular axes with ticks only on the bottom and left, black filled square measurements, a thin red fitted line, large black axis labels and tick labels, no grid or legend, and `K` with its **actual units** plus `R²` inside the lower-right plot. Label the x-axis `Concentration` and the y-axis `Δcurrent` or `Δcurrent density` according to the input. Never copy the example's numeric K or R². The Origin runner writes this style into the OPJU before PNG/PDF export and verifies both annotation labels survive reopening. After Origin verifies the native linear fit, `scripts/reference_figure.py` additionally generates a matching `sensitivity_reference_style.svg` and `.png`; retain these alongside the Origin OPJU and exports. Do not present a default-style Origin export as an equivalent calibration figure. Open **each** delivered image and PDF to check its frame and visible K/R² before delivery. If input is A/cm² and the desired displayed unit is µA/cm², set `source_current_unit`, `current_scale: 1000000`, and `current_unit: "µA/cm²"` in the manifest so both Origin data and figure labels use the scaled values without dividing by area again.

## Run

Read [the manifest format](references/manifest.md) and construct a JSON manifest from the user's files. Use an explicitly selected 64-bit Windows Python 3.11 interpreter with the dependencies in [requirements.txt](requirements.txt), preferably a dedicated virtual environment; verify its version and bitness before running. Resolve the script relative to this skill's actual installation directory rather than assuming a particular username or drive. See the repository README for installation and the clearly labelled synthetic example under `examples/synthetic/` for a software smoke test. For each single-peak analysis, create **one new, human-readable case folder** under the requested delivery parent (for example `HVA_五浓度_峰形质控_2026-09-29`). Put the manifest, raw-curve/baseline overlay review plot, `baseline_choice.md`, summary, and the helper's `run_*` subfolder together inside that case folder; pass the case folder as `--output-root`. Do not scatter sibling files in the delivery parent. Keep output outside the skill and original source files.

```powershell
& '<absolute path to your Python 3.11 environment>/python.exe' '<actual skill directory>/scripts/sensitivity.py' --manifest '<absolute manifest.json>' --output-root '<absolute output folder>'
```

Use `--prepare-only` for inspection, planning, or provisional multi-peak folder ordering; it validates/extracts without opening Origin and labels the Python fit as a cross-check, not the final Origin result. For normal execution, the helper invokes the bundled Origin runner. It writes a `run_*` folder containing processed curves, `measurements.csv`, `calibration.csv`, `figure_spec.json`, `analysis.json`, and the runner's Origin output and QA log. The original inputs remain untouched.

After a successful, verified Origin run, put the standard calibration PNG, an additional clearly labelled native Origin export, and the editable OPJU **directly in the top level of each case folder**: the reference-style calibration PNG (`sensitivity_reference_style.png`) and the editable native Origin OPJU from `run_*/output/opju/`. Use descriptive names such as `标准标定图_<peak>.png` and `Origin可编辑工程_<peak>.opju`; keep the original files inside `run_*` so recorded paths and QA evidence remain valid. In addition to the two existing copies, add `Origin原生导出图_<peak>.png` from the verified native Origin PNG (not from the external reference-style renderer). Check that all top-level copies are nonempty and byte-identical to their respective verified originals, and give the user their exact filenames and case-folder path in the final response. For multiple qualifying peaks, do this in every peak's case folder. Do not label an OPJU as validated when Origin or its reopening check failed.

Before reporting results, inspect the baseline overlay and `baseline_choice.md`, then `peak_quality.json`, `analysis.json`, and (for a passing analysis) the Origin QA log. Open both the Origin export and the reference-style PNG, and verify the OPJU, SVG, PNG, and PDF exist and the displayed K/R² agrees with `analysis.json`. Inspect `curve_*.csv` and `measurements.csv` to check the selected peak or trough and baseline; replicate SD is retained in `calibration.csv` but is not drawn as an error bar. Confirm those choices are scientifically plausible; numerical QA alone cannot do that. The helper cross-checks Origin's K and intercept against an independent Python least-squares calculation. If quality screening fails, deliver the quality report and raw-curve review without reporting a sensitivity. If Origin fails, report that no validated Origin result was produced and keep the prepared files for diagnosis.
